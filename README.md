**Thesis Statement**: Due to the rising levels in PM 2.5 during July-September, Boston residents are responsible and must take initiate in using their individuals power to lower these concentration levels. <br><br>
**Context**: PM 2.5 is a particle that includes chemicals, dust, soot, and metals. PM 2.5 generally comes from substances like gasoline, oil, diesel fuel, etc. 
These particles contribute to premature death with those with heart or lung disease, increased respiratory symptoms, nonfatal heart attacks, etc. As shown PM 2.5 can contribute significantly to human life and quality of it.  <br><br>
**Who**: The general public of Boston, MA who are interested in protecting the wellbeing of themselves and others. In terms of myself, I am a Computer Science student at Westmont College who has taken an interest in Environmental Justice. <br> <br>
**What**: *Action*: Looking at the data, there is a fluctuation between the months of July and September. This is due to those being the warmest months in Boston therefore the PM 2.5 Concentration levels will fluctuate to elevated numbers like the 20’s. This exceeds the normal/amount of 12. This is a call to action that, during the months of July - September, Boston locals need to be aware of how they are personally contributing to this rise. To reduce this concentration value, I encourage Boston locals to utilize public transportation instead of using their own individual cars to lower gas emissions. Though this may be a large purchase, investing in electric cars will reduce gasoline emissions significantly. Another action item includes using more environmentally friendly household appliances.<br><br>
*Mechanism*:  I will be using a live presentation to communicate my data with my audience. This will allow me full control on the narrative of my observations and data. <br><br>
*Tone*: The topic I will be discussing is a generally serious topic as it may seem like a colossal issue to fix and does personally affect individuals in their health. The tone I will be carrying throughout my presentation parallel to this is one of thoughtfulness to express the severity and significance of this situation. But I also plan to add a hopeful tone through my call to actions, showing that there is a way to stop the increasing PM 2.5 Concentration level through individual action. <br><br>
**How**: This is shown through the line graph that displays all the PM 2.5 concentration data from stations in the Boston area over the year 2024. A line graph will be most useful in adding to the narrative and story I am trying to push because it shows the concentration levels through a series of times and it clearly shows when levels rose or decreased from the recommended PM 2.5 value.<br><br>
This dashboard also incorporates presentation techniques like the avoidance of clutter. I focused on minimalism with my dashboard as I wanted to show the most necessary components that would allow me to share my story. Most of the data can speak for itself, but I added a reference point to show how significantly larger the PM 2.5 concentration is compared to the recommended amount. I avoided a large amount of words and access to color as that has been shown to distract and confuse viewers as well as dilute the overall message.
